# Product: Scope

A web application for a car service station that replaces Excel and paper logs: customers, vehicles, work orders,
services, parts, payments. It keeps the history of every vehicle and gives a transparent order lifecycle.

Details: [entities](../domain/entities.md), [status transitions](../domain/work-order-lifecycle.md), [operations and access](../domain/operations.md).

## In scope

- Customers and vehicles, with soft delete
- Employees with roles `MECHANIC` and `MANAGER`, login by password
- Price lists of services and parts
- Work orders with lines, status lifecycle and status history
- Payments (one full payment per order)
- Search and order list filters
- Role-based access

## Postponed

Decide after the main "what" and "how" questions are settled:

- Photos of vehicles and work orders
- Customer cabinet: the customer sees the current status of the vehicle. It needs customer accounts; a cheaper option is
  a status page opened by a secret link, without accounts

## Out of scope

- Customer as a system user (a customer is only a record)
- Invoice as a separate entity (the printed view of an order serves as the invoice)
- Warehouse and stock accounting (parts are only a price list)
- Employee rate
- Per-job time stamps for mechanics (the status history shows when the order paused and became ready)
- Audit of amount changes (only status changes and who recorded a payment are stored)
- Partial payments and refunds
- Integration with payment systems (a manager records every payment by hand)
- Team of mechanics per order (one responsible mechanic plus a performer per line)
- History of vehicle owners (an order keeps the customer at the time of the visit)
- Second database for order history

## Prohibitions

- Do not implement anything listed under Postponed or Out of scope. If a task seems to need it, ask.
- Do not edit this file without human approval.
